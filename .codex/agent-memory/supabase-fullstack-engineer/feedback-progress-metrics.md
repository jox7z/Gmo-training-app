name: feedback-progress-metrics
description: Progress UI must use understandable real-session data, not estimated strength formulas or guaranteed-improvement framing.
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Progress tracking must use actual completed-set data: load, repetitions, work volume, and comparable active duration. Never expose Epley, Brzycki, estimated 1RM, or arbitrary 12-repetition prescriptions.

**Why:** Most users will not understand formula jargon, estimates look more authoritative than they are, and users can improve, remain stable, or decline.

**How to apply:** Plot the real session series without labeling one session as
improved, equal or declined. Offer work (`load × reps`), top load, total reps and
recorded active time as separate factual views; label work `kg·rep`/`lb·rep`, not
as plain weight. Reuse the workout validation bounds for legacy/remote sets. Keep user body-weight tracking in
its own “Peso corporal” section. A flat or falling trend is valid and must remain
visible.
