# GMO fitness social art direction

## Brand stance

- Build from the GMO robot identity: disciplined, competitive, human, dark, and direct.
- Use cream for readable warmth (`text.primary` is `#F6F2EC`, never pure white), black for the training environment, and the `colors.ember` ramp for decisive emphasis.
- Ember is a colour **temperature**, not a light source. Keep glow restrained (`glow` tokens sit at 0.22): structure comes from the surface ramp, hairline borders and `gradients.emberEdge` rims — never from bloom.
- Make effort and recorded performance feel tangible. Keep the tone factual; never manufacture praise, shame, diagnosis, or performance verdicts.
- Treat the robot mark and mascot as recognizable anchors, not decorative wallpaper.

## Direct the composition

- Choose one visual thesis per surface: training ledger, competitive rank, earned achievement, community moment, or editorial story.
- Establish hierarchy with scale, crop, typography, negative space, and one dominant focal point.
- Use industrial cues sparingly: stamped labels, scorekeeping grids, hard separators, restrained grain, and training-floor photography.
- Keep data legible over decoration. Use red to focus attention, not to color every state.
- Give ranks and achievements ceremony without making ordinary screens feel like game menus.
- Follow social composition geometry from `gmo-mobile-product-design`; use art direction to strengthen its focal point and image treatment.
- A rank promotion is an earned full-width moment: real crest, restrained
  rank-colored halo, hard horizontal separators and `NUEVO RANGO`. Invalid or
  downward rank metadata gets a neutral factual treatment, never borrowed ceremony.
- Keep personal identity compact: rank strip, 72 px avatar, small crest, short
  factual lines and canonical social posts. Do not turn the profile into a
  full-screen player card.
- Present routine planning as `GMO Rating`: one segmented radial, `/100`, compact
  2×2 legend and one limitation line. Do not add coaching copy or verdicts.

## Reject

- Avoid generic neon fitness, chrome bodybuilding clichés, flame effects, fake sweat overlays, crypto-game emblems, and motivational-poster copy.
- Avoid soft wellness pastels, purple AI gradients, generic glass cards, and stock-dashboard visuals.
- Do not invent exercise demonstrations, physiological claims, transformation promises, fake statistics, or endorsements.
- Do not replace the shipped rank crest system with unrelated iconography.

## Deliver

- Name the concept and define palette roles, type behavior, image treatment, composition rules, and reusable motifs.
- Show how the concept scales across at least a dense data surface and a social/celebratory surface.
- Hand native layout decisions to `gmo-mobile-product-design`, animation to `gmo-motion-language`, and production assets to `gmo-mobile-assets`.
